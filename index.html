The collective, on set 月

Under the
same moon.

Getsu (月) means moon. We're a small collective of photographers and filmmakers who believe the best work happens between disciplines — a documentary eye on a brand film, a cinematographer's patience on a stills day.

The same sensibility runs through our clothing line: quiet, considered pieces made in small runs, photographed by us, worn on our sets.

01 Listen Every project starts with a conversation, not a treatment. We learn your audience, your constraints, your ambition.
02 Direct Treatment, boards, casting, location. You see exactly what we'll shoot before a single frame rolls.
03 Craft Shoot days run photo and film in parallel — one crew, one look, twice the material.
04 Deliver Edit, grade, and every cut-down your channels need. Delivered on time, organised, ready to publish.
